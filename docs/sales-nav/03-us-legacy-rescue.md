# Sales Navigator — US Legacy Rescue

**Lead list name:** `US — Legacy rescue`  
**Goal:** Teams with broken or overloaded codebases (matches **Legacy rescue** at $3k/week).

## Search type

**Lead search** (people)

## Filters

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Geography | United States |
| Seniority | Director, VP, CXO, Owner, Partner |
| Current job title | CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product, Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Director of Engineering |
| Company headcount | 11–200 |
| Industry | Software Development, Computer Software, Financial Services, E-Learning Providers, Technology/Internet |
| Profile language | English |
| Keywords | `legacy` OR `rewrite` OR `refactor` OR `technical debt` OR `production issues` OR `migration` OR `firefighting` |
| Spotlights | Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days (priority — people venting about code are warm) |
| Relationship | 2nd-degree connections |

## Exclude

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Industry | Staffing and Recruiting, IT Services and IT Consulting (large consultancies) |
| Keywords | `recruiting`, `staff augmentation` |

## Qualification checklist

- [ ] Has an **existing product in production** (not greenfield MVP)
- [ ] Recent post or comment about bugs, rewrites, slow delivery, or hiring senior help
- [ ] Decision-maker or direct report to CEO with eng budget influence
- [ ] Not looking for 10 cheap offshore devs — needs judgment and shipping

## Outreach angle

**Fix, don't rewrite** unless necessary. Weekly shipped value. Enterprise background (fintech, 60+ microservices, observability) as credibility for messy systems.

## Example opener

> Hi [Name] — saw your post about [technical debt / production issues / rewrite]. I do legacy rescue as a solo dev — fix what's broken, cut dead weight, ship something useful every week ($3k/week). No pitch call if you want to walk through the codebase first.

## Weekly target

Add **10–20 qualified leads** per week from this search.
