# Daily routine — solo client acquisition

**Time budget:** 45–60 minutes on weekdays (skip or halve on weekends).  
**Tools:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, lead lists, Calendly, [work-with-me](https://guseyn.com/html/work-with-me.html), simple tracker (spreadsheet or Notion).

---

## Morning block (25–30 min) — Sales Navigator

### 1. Open today's search (rotate)

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| [01 — US Founders MVP](./01-us-founders-mvp.md) | [02 — SaaS eng leaders](./02-us-saas-eng-leaders.md) | [03 — Legacy rescue](./03-us-legacy-rescue.md) | [04 — Account EdTech/FinTech](./04-us-account-edtech-fintech.md) | Catch-up: best-performing list |

### 2. Filter for fresh leads

- Sort or spotlight: **Changed in last 7 days** or **Posted in last 30 days**
- Review **15–20 profiles**

### 3. Qualify each lead

Use the checklist in that day's search doc. Skip:

- Recruiters and staffing agencies  
- Dev shops selling services (your competitors)  
- Companies with no product and no recent activity  

### 4. Save to lead list

Add qualified leads to the named list (e.g. `US — Founders — MVP`).

### 5. Send outreach

- **5–10 personalized messages** per day (connection request note or InMail)
- No rate in first message
- One proof point + one CTA (Calendly or work-with-me)
- Log: name, company, date, search source, status

---

## Midday block (10 min) — Engage before you pitch

- Comment thoughtfully on **2–3 posts** from people in your lead lists (same day or from yesterday's saves)
- Accept connection requests; send follow-up only after they accept (unless InMail)

---

## Afternoon block (10–15 min) — Pipeline & content

### Monday / Wednesday / Friday — Pipeline

- Reply to all inbound (email, Telegram, LinkedIn) within **24 hours**
- Move leads in tracker: `Saved → Contacted → Replied → Call booked → Proposal → Won/Lost`
- Follow up on conversations with no reply after **5–7 days** (one short bump only)

### Tuesday / Thursday — LinkedIn visibility

Pick one:

- Short post with a **demo clip** (InstruxMusic, Unison, or a shipped feature)
- Share a blog post from guseyn.com with one practical takeaway
- Repost with comment on startup/MVP/legacy topic

**Do not** post only "I'm available for hire." Show work.

---

## Weekly review (Friday, +15 min)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Leads reviewed | 75–100 |
| Leads saved | 30–50 |
| Outbound messages sent | 25–50 |
| Replies | 2–5 |
| Discovery calls booked | 0–2 |

Questions to ask:

1. Which saved search got the most replies? Do more of that next week.  
2. Did anyone visit work-with-me or book Calendly? (check analytics / Calendly)  
3. Any referral ask to send to InstruxMusic / past contacts?

---

## Monthly (once)

- Refresh LinkedIn headline and Featured (work-with-me, Calendly, demo video)
- Ask one happy client for a LinkedIn recommendation if you don't have a recent one
- Prune lead lists: remove wrong-fit contacts

---

## Message templates (adapt — never copy-paste blindly)

**After connection accepted:**

> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I saw [specific thing about their company/post]. I work solo with one US client at a time — recently shipped InstruxMusic (scheduling, Stripe, WebRTC). If you're ever weighing build vs hire vs agency, happy to do a free 30-min discovery call: [Calendly link]

**Follow-up (5–7 days, no reply):**

> Hi [Name] — bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure — if timing's wrong, just say so. If useful, here's how I work: guseyn.com/html/work-with-me.html

---

## What not to do

- Mass generic InMails with your rate  
- More than 20 connection requests/day (LinkedIn limits / quality drop)  
- Argue about stack on first contact  
- Wait for inbound only — the work-with-me page converts; Sales Nav generates traffic

---

## Success expectations (realistic)

- **Month 1:** Lists built, rhythm established, few replies — normal  
- **Month 2–3:** 3–8 discovery calls total if consistent  
- **Signed client:** Often 1 per 200–400 targeted touches; referrals close faster

Stay on the routine for **at least 8 weeks** before judging whether a search is "not working."
