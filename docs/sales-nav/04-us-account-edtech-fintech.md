# Sales Navigator — US Accounts (EdTech / FinTech / Music)

**Lead list name:** `US — Accounts — EdTech FinTech Music`  
**Goal:** Find **companies** first, then add the right decision-maker inside (account-based outreach).

## Search type

**Account search** (companies) → then **Lead search** from account page

## Account filters

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Geography | United States |
| Company headcount | 1–50 |
| Industry | E-Learning Providers, Financial Services, Software Development, Musicians, Performing Arts |
| Keywords | `SaaS` OR `platform` OR `scheduling` OR `payments` OR `music` OR `education` OR `fintech` |
| Headcount growth | Growing (if available) |
| Relationship | 2nd-degree connections at account |

## Why these industries

Matches your proof: **InstruxMusic** (EdTech/music, USA), enterprise **fintech** background, **Unison** (music tech). Easier to open conversations when you speak their domain.

## After you find an account

1. Open company page in Sales Navigator  
2. Click **Decision makers** or **People**  
3. Filter roles: Founder, CEO, CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product  
4. Check recent activity (posts, job changes, funding news)  
5. Add qualified person to the matching lead list (MVP, SaaS, or Legacy)

## Qualification checklist

- [ ] Product company in ed-tech, music, fintech, or B2B SaaS
- [ ] 1–50 employees (large enough to pay, small enough to need solo senior help)
- [ ] Active website or app (not dormant shell company)
- [ ] At least one decision-maker with recent LinkedIn activity

## Outreach angle

Domain-specific hook beats generic dev pitch.

**EdTech / music:** InstruxMusic — scheduling, Stripe, WebRTC classrooms, white-label.  
**FinTech:** Enterprise payment systems, Azure AD auth, 60+ microservices, observability.  
**SaaS:** Solo MVP speed, one owner for whole stack.

## Example opener (EdTech)

> Hi [Name] — I build software for music schools (InstruxMusic — scheduling, billing, online lessons). Saw [Company] is in a similar space. I'm a solo full-stack dev, one US client at a time. Open to a quick call if you're expanding the product.

## Weekly target

Research **10–15 accounts** per week; add **5–10 decision-makers** to lead lists.
