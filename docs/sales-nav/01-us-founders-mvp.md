# Sales Navigator — US Founders (MVP)

**Lead list name:** `US — Founders — MVP`  
**Goal:** Non-technical or semi-technical founders who need a solo builder for 0→1 (matches **MVP to production** at $10k/month).

## Search type

**Lead search** (people)

## Filters

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Geography | United States |
| Seniority | Owner, CXO, Partner |
| Current job title | Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Owner, Managing Director |
| Company headcount | 1–10 (expand to 11–50 if list is too small) |
| Industry | Software Development, Technology/Internet/Information, E-Learning Providers, Financial Services, Musicians |
| Profile language | English |
| Keywords | `MVP` OR `SaaS` OR `startup` OR `bootstrap` OR `product launch` OR `no CTO` OR `looking for developer` |
| Spotlights | Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days; Changed jobs in past 90 days |
| Relationship | 2nd-degree connections (prioritize); TeamLink connections if available |

## Exclude

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Industry | Staffing and Recruiting |
| Company headcount | 1001+ |
| Keywords in title | `recruiter`, `staffing`, `talent acquisition` |

## Qualification checklist (before adding to list)

- [ ] Company is building a **product** (not an agency selling dev services)
- [ ] Posted or commented about product, hiring, or launch in the last 60 days
- [ ] Not a recruiter or dev shop owner
- [ ] Problem fits: new SaaS, booking app, internal tool, MVP, no full eng team yet

## Outreach angle

Reference their product stage. Proof points: InstruxMusic MVP in under 3 months, Stripe, WebRTC, solo ownership. CTA: free discovery call ([Calendly](https://calendly.com/guseyn-guseyn/first-call)) or [work-with-me](https://guseyn.com/html/work-with-me.html).

## Example opener

> Hi [Name] — saw you're building [product/company]. I'm a solo full-stack dev (currently on InstruxMusic — scheduling, Stripe, video classrooms) and take one US client at a time for MVP → production. Happy to do a no-pitch discovery call if that's useful.

## Weekly target

Add **20–30 qualified leads** per week from this search.
