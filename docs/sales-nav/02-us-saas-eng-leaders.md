# Sales Navigator — US SaaS Engineering Leaders

**Lead list name:** `US — SaaS — Eng leaders`  
**Goal:** Small SaaS companies that need senior solo capacity for build + ongoing work ($10k build / $6k maintain).

## Search type

**Lead search** (people)

## Filters

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Geography | United States |
| Seniority | Director, VP, CXO, Partner |
| Current job title | CTO, VP Engineering, VP of Engineering, Head of Engineering, Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering |
| Company headcount | 11–50 (sweet spot); optionally 51–200 for maintenance/rescue |
| Industry | Software Development, Computer Software, Technology/Internet |
| Profile language | English |
| Keywords | `SaaS` OR `platform` OR `technical debt` OR `roadmap` OR `hire engineer` OR `Stripe` OR `React` OR `Node` |
| Technologies used | Stripe, AWS, React (if available in your plan) |
| Spotlights | Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days |
| Relationship | 2nd-degree connections |

## Exclude

| Filter | Setting |
|--------|---------|
| Industry | IT Services and IT Consulting, Staffing and Recruiting |
| Company headcount | 1001+ |
| Current job title | Recruiter, Talent Acquisition |

## Qualification checklist

- [ ] B2B product company (not consultancy body shop)
- [ ] Eng team exists but is small (likely 2–15 engineers)
- [ ] Signs of active product work: job posts, release notes, eng blog, funding news
- [ ] Good fit for: fractional senior engineer, feature delivery, infra simplification

## Outreach angle

Lead with **ownership and speed**, not rate. Mention you replace agency overhead — one person for frontend, backend, deploy. Link demos: InstruxMusic desktop/mobile on work-with-me page.

## Example opener

> Hi [Name] — noticed [Company] is scaling [product area]. I work as a solo full-stack dev with one client at a time — shipped InstruxMusic (scheduling, Stripe, WebRTC) solo in under 3 months. If you ever need senior capacity without agency overhead, happy to chat.

## Weekly target

Add **15–25 qualified leads** per week from this search.
